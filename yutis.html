Vedic Yutis · Reference

A Yuti (conjunction) is formed when two numbers are adjacent in the Vedic Lo Shu grid. Each pair of planets carries a specific energy — positive, virodhi (conflicting), conditional, or neutral.

Positive — harmonious energy
Virodhi — conflicting energy
Conditional — context-dependent
Neutral — mild or balanced
